PHOTOS TO PDF
=============

Turns photos of letters and documents (Dutch or English) into tidy,
searchable PDF files with clear names, sorted into folders.

Everything happens on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, it works
without internet, and your photos are never moved, changed or deleted.


THE MAIN THING: ORGANIZE A FOLDER OF PHOTOS
-------------------------------------------

1. Open Photos to PDF and click "Organize a folder of photos".
   Choose the folder where your photos are. Folders inside it are
   included too.
   (Or: drag the folder onto the app window, or right-click the folder >
   Send to > Photos to PDF.)

2. Wait while it looks through your photos. It finds the letters and
   documents, straightens pages photographed at an angle, turns them the
   right way up and reads their date and sender.
   This takes a second or two per photo, so a big folder can take a while.
   Your computer won't go to sleep meanwhile. You can press Stop at any
   time: the next time you open the same folder, it continues where it
   left off.

3. Check what it found. Each document shows its pages and a name such as
       2025-03-14 Belastingdienst - Voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting 2025
   - Click a page to see it large.
   - The small buttons under a page turn it left or right, start a new
     document from that page, or remove it.
   - Click the name to change it.
   - "Join with document before" puts two documents back together.
   - "Not a document" leaves something out.
   - Untick "Save this document" to skip it for now.
   - Made a mistake? Press Undo.
   - Under "Not documents" you'll find the photos it left out. Click one
     if it is actually a document.

4. Press Save. The PDFs go into a folder called "PDFs" inside the folder
   you chose, sorted into one folder per year (you can change this at the
   bottom of the window: per year, per sender, or all together).

Do it again whenever you like. Documents already saved are remembered and
won't be saved twice: they appear under "Already saved". New photos show
up as new documents.


FOR ONE LETTER: MAKE ONE PDF FROM A FEW PHOTOS
----------------------------------------------

Click "Make one PDF from a few photos" and pick the photos of the pages.
(Or drag the photos onto the window, or select them in File Explorer,
right-click > Send to > Photos to PDF.)
One PDF appears next to the photos, named from the letter when possible.
On Windows 11, "Send to" is under "Show more options".


SETTINGS
--------

The recommended settings are already chosen. In Settings you can change:
- how the PDFs are arranged (per year, per sender, both, or all together)
- whether folders inside the chosen folder are included
- one PDF for all photos, or one PDF per photo (for the "few photos" option)
- searchable PDFs, straightening, and black-and-white "scan" look


TIPS
----

- Straightening works best when all four corners of the page are in the
  photo and the page stands out from what it's lying on. Curved pages
  (open books) and folded pages are not flattened.
- iPhone photos (.HEIC) work as they are.
- If a name is wrong, just type a better one before pressing Save.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG, OR YOU HAVE AN IDEA
--------------------------------------------

Click Help > "Report a problem or idea...". Type what happened (or what
you'd like), click "Create report", and a .zip file appears on your
Desktop. Attach it to an email to the person who set up this app.

The report contains the app's own notes about what it did (which photos
it looked at, how long things took, any errors) and your settings. It
does not contain your photos, your PDFs or the text of your letters. You
choose whether to include the dates, senders and document types the app
recognised. Nothing is sent automatically.

The notes are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Photo2PDF\logs (Help > "Open the log
folder") and never take more than about 6 MB.
