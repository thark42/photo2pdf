PHOTOS TO PDF
=============

Turns photos of documents into PDF files. Everything happens on this
computer: nothing is uploaded anywhere, and it works without internet.


THREE WAYS TO USE IT
--------------------

1. Right-click
   Select one or more photos in File Explorer, right-click,
   choose "Send to" > "Photos to PDF".
   (On Windows 11, click "Show more options" first to see "Send to".)

2. Drag and drop
   Drag photos (or a whole folder) onto the "Photos to PDF" icon on the
   desktop, or into the app window.

3. Open the app
   Start menu > "Photos to PDF", then click the big box and pick photos.

The PDF is saved in the same folder as the photos, and that folder opens
automatically with the new PDF highlighted.


FIND THE DOCUMENTS IN A FOLDER OF PHOTOS
----------------------------------------

Got a folder with a mix of holiday snaps and photos of letters, bills or
receipts? Click "Find the documents in a folder of photos..." (or drag
the folder into the window, or right-click the folder > Send to >
Photos to PDF).

The app looks at every photo and picks out the ones with a page of text
in them. Photos taken within a few minutes of each other become one
multi-page PDF; a gap, or an ordinary photo in between, starts a new one.

You then get a screen showing what it found:
- Click a page to move it left/right, start a new document from it, or
  remove it.
- "Join with document above" merges two documents into one.
- "Not a document" drops a whole group.
- Under "Other photos", click a photo that the app missed to add it.

Click "Save" and the PDFs go into a "PDFs" folder inside the photo folder
(you can change this). Nothing is moved or deleted; your photos stay as
they are.

Checking takes about a second per photo, so a big folder takes a while.
Doing the same folder again later is much quicker.


OPTIONS (in the app window; remembered for next time)
-----------------------------------------------------

- One PDF for all photos, or a separate PDF for each photo.
  Pages are ordered by file name, which for phone photos is the order
  they were taken.
- Make text searchable: lets you search the PDF and copy text out of it.
  Also turns sideways or upside-down pages the right way up.
  Takes a few seconds per page.
- Black and white: evens out shadows so the page looks like a scan, and
  makes smaller files. Best for plain text documents.


TIPS FOR BETTER RESULTS
-----------------------

- Photograph the page straight from above, in good light, filling the
  frame. The app does not straighten pages photographed at an angle.
- iPhone photos (.HEIC) work as they are.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

A log file is kept at:  %APPDATA%\Photo2PDF\log.txt
(paste that into the File Explorer address bar to find it).
