PHOTOS TO PDF
=============

Turns photos of documents (Dutch or English) into searchable PDF files.
Everything happens on this computer: nothing is uploaded anywhere, and it
works without internet.


THREE WAYS TO USE IT
--------------------

1. Right-click
   Select one or more photos in File Explorer, right-click,
   choose "Send to" > "Photos to PDF".
   (On Windows 11, click "Show more options" first to see "Send to".)

2. Drag and drop
   Drag photos (or a whole folder) onto the "Photos to PDF" icon on the
   desktop, or into the app window.

3. Open the app
   Start menu > "Photos to PDF", then click the big box and pick photos.

The PDF is saved in the same folder as the photos, and that folder opens
automatically with the new PDF highlighted.


FIND THE DOCUMENTS IN A FOLDER OF PHOTOS
----------------------------------------

Got a folder with a mix of holiday snaps and photos of letters, bills or
receipts? Click "Find the documents in a folder of photos..." (or drag
the folder into the window, or right-click the folder > Send to >
Photos to PDF).

Tick "include its subfolders" first if your photos are spread over
several folders.

The app looks at every photo and picks out the ones with a page of text
in them. It straightens pages that were photographed at an angle and
turns sideways or upside-down pages the right way up. Photos taken within
a few minutes of each other become one multi-page PDF; a gap, an ordinary
photo in between, or a page from a different sender starts a new one.

It also reads each letter to suggest a file name, for example:
   2025-03-14 Belastingdienst - Voorlopige aanslag inkomstenbelasting 2025
When the date, sender or type isn't clear, it uses a plain name like
"Document 03 - 2025-03-15 10.15 (2 pages)" instead.

You then get a screen showing what it found:
- Click a page to rotate it, move it left/right, start a new document
  from it, or remove it.
- Click a file name to change it.
- "Join with document above" merges two documents into one.
- "Not a document" drops a whole group.
- Under "Other photos", click a photo that the app missed to add it.

Click "Save" and the PDFs go into a "PDFs" folder inside the photo folder
(you can change this). Nothing is moved or deleted; your photos stay as
they are.

Checking takes about a second per photo, so a big folder takes a while.
Doing the same folder again later is much quicker.


OPTIONS (in the app window; remembered for next time)
-----------------------------------------------------

- One PDF for all photos, or a separate PDF for each photo.
  Pages are ordered by file name, which for phone photos is the order
  they were taken.
- Make text searchable: lets you search the PDF and copy text out of it.
  Also turns sideways or upside-down pages the right way up.
  Takes a few seconds per page.
- Black and white: evens out shadows so the page looks like a scan, and
  makes smaller files. Best for plain text documents.


TIPS FOR BETTER RESULTS
-----------------------

- Pages photographed at an angle are straightened automatically, as long
  as all four corners of the page are in the photo and the page stands
  out from what it's lying on. Curved pages (open books) and folded pages
  are not flattened.
- iPhone photos (.HEIC) work as they are.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG, OR YOU HAVE AN IDEA
--------------------------------------------

In the app, click Help > "Report a problem or idea...". Type what happened
(or what you'd like), click "Create report", and a .zip file appears on
your Desktop. Attach it to an email to the person who set up this app.

When the app runs into a problem it offers to make this report for you.

The report contains the app's own notes about what it did (which photos it
looked at, how long things took, any errors) and your settings. It does
not contain your photos, your PDFs or the text of your letters. You can
choose whether to include the dates, senders and document types the app
recognised. Nothing is sent automatically.

The notes themselves are kept in:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Photo2PDF\logs
(Help > "Open the log folder"). Old notes are deleted automatically, so
they never take more than about 6 MB.
