Offline Windows app that turns phone photos of documents into named, searchable PDFs (Dutch and English).

**Download `PhotosToPDF-Setup-<version>.exe`** below and run it. No admin rights needed. It adds Start menu, desktop and "Send to" shortcuts. Instructions: `HOW_TO_USE.txt` (installed alongside the app).

- The installer is not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen will warn on first run: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- `PhotosToPDF-portable-<version>.zip` is the same app as an unzip-and-run folder.
- Everything runs on your computer; the app makes no network connections.
- Third-party licences are in the `licenses` folder next to the app.
